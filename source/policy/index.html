Privacy Policy

Authenticator PWA — last updated 2025

Short version: Authenticator stores everything on your device and collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no servers of ours involved.

Data storage

All data — OTP accounts, secrets, settings, and encrypted backups — is stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage / IndexedDB). Nothing is transmitted to us; we have no servers that receive your data.

Data collection

We do not collect, log, or share any personal information. The app contains no analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising of any kind.

Encryption and verification

If you set a passphrase, your stored secrets are encrypted on-device (Argon2 + AES). If you enable biometric verification, the WebAuthn prompt is handled entirely by your browser and operating system — we never see your biometric data.

Permissions

The app may request camera access solely to scan QR codes when you choose to import an account. Video frames are processed locally in the page and never leave your device. Clipboard access is used only to copy a code when you click one.

Network activity

The app works fully offline after install (a service worker caches the app shell). Network requests occur only to fetch updated app files from our hosting (Cloudflare Pages) and — only if you explicitly sign in — to Microsoft's services for optional OneDrive backup. Backups you export stay under your control.

Browser storage warning

Browser storage is not guaranteed to be durable: clearing site data, freeing disk space, or uninstalling can erase it. Keep a backup of your codes somewhere you trust (Menu → Backup).
